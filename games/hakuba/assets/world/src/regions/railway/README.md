# railway

The Belmonte railway between Hidamari and Belmonte, standing still: its track in hundred-metre stretches with their
sleepers, the tunnels' portals and the ends of their linings, the viaducts, banks, buffer stops, stop boards and
turnouts, Belmonte's station and the road's overbridge, and two trains, one waiting at each terminus.
`placements.json` stands each of its props at its map position, height, turn and scale.
