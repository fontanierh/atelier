# craterpark

Crater Park, the concrete and wooden skate park on the caldera floor of the volcano. `placements.json` stands each of its props at its map position, height, turn and scale.

The placements also own 28 coping lines, six assistance boxes, twelve boost boxes and physical material rules. Their source identity is `b310758e512f44f5def9b5507e05404b1144f3b682d50cb285915c15d76e3d0a`; every cell origin matches this geometry within the 0.1 mm stored-placement rounding.
