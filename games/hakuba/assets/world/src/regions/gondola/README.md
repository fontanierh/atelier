# gondola

The three gondola lines, standing still: Hidamari to the southeast rim of the caldera, Belmonte to its northwest rim,
and the caldera floor to its own rim station. Each station stands on timber posts with stone feet, with its stair down
to the ground; the timber towers carry the two haul ropes, in stretches of about 60 m; the cabins hang plumb from their
grips, each grip on its rope. `placements.json` stands each of its props at its map position, height, turn and scale.
