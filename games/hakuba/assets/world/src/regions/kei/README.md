# kei

The four kei cars parked in the Mega Park's car park: a pickup with a crate and a skateboard in its bed, a wagon, a
retro car and a microvan with two skateboards on its roof rack. Each is painted per vertex and is solid with its box
hulls. A car stands a few degrees out of upright on the car park's slope, and that tilt is in its own mesh;
`placements.json` stands each car at its map position, height, heading and scale.
