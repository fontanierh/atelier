# Roadside camp

`layout.rs` places the fire, curtain, weapon rack and supplies around the camp centre, facing the road. The coast's
published vegetation keeps the 15.5 m clearing around that centre.

The four contact records are independent measurements against the original source terrain triangles, using the
source model geometry with its 2 cm seating offset. The fire origin agrees with a retained original game log. The
other three origins and the logical owners hit by the original visibility traces have not been independently verified;
their terrain-based contact values are estimates rather than confirmed runtime trace results. The source-contact
contract's logical-owner requirement remains unverified for these placements.
