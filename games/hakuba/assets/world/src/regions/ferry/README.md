# ferry

The passenger ferry between Hidamari and Brinecove, docked at Hidamari's berth, and its two terminals: Hidamari's
jetty, stage, brow, waiting house and timetable, and Brinecove's landing, brow, pavilion, ticket hut, fenders and
quayside props. `placements.json` stands each of its props at its map position, height, turn and scale.
