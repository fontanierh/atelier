What gets saved?

Every hand and finger joint is recorded relative to the steering wheel. The right hand is generated from the left using this character’s own skeleton. Steering moves the saved grip as a whole; it never changes your finger settings.

Saves stay on this machine, separately for each character. Earlier saves remain recoverable.
