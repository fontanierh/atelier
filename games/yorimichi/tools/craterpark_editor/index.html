Controls

Look aroundDrag empty space to orbit · right-drag (or two fingers) to pan · scroll to zoom. T top view, V 3D view, F frame the selection.
SelectClick a piece. Shift-click adds or removes pieces. Double-click a piece selects its whole line (or click the line in the Lines list). Esc clears.
MoveDrag a piece (or the whole selection). Pieces attached behind it follow. Near another piece’s end it snaps on (orange ring). Arrows nudge 1 m, with Shift 10 m.
Raise / lowerAlt-drag up and down, or PgUp/PgDn (or ]/[) 0.5 m, with Shift 5 m. G puts the piece on the ground.
RotateDrag the white ring, or Q/E 5°, with Shift 15°. A whole line rotates about its centre.
ShapeEvery dimension is in the right-hand panel: drag a slider or type a value. Roll-ins: drop, steepness, top and bottom curves, bend left or right. Runways: length, turn, slope, walls.
GapsSelect a jump, type the gap you want, press Fit kicker and landing: it picks the lip angle, kicker radius, knuckle drop and landing for the speed riders actually arrive with, and says if there isn’t enough speed and how much more drop would fix it. Or load a proven module.
Ride itSelect a piece and press R (or ▶ Ride under the view) to watch the slowest rider go down that line, with a chase camera.
AddPick from the left. With a piece selected the new one is attached to its end (and inserted before what followed); otherwise click the crater to drop it.
ConnectDrag a piece’s start onto another piece’s end to attach it. In the panel, “Rider goes to” lists where the line continues; “Link to…” then click a piece adds a branch or a jump target.
Buried pieces“See-through ground” (X) makes the crater translucent. Riding surfaces below the ground are tinted red.
One line onlyIn Lines: the eye hides a line, Solo shows only that line.
Copy / delete⌘D duplicates, Delete removes. ⌘Z undo, ⇧⌘Z redo.
Working with ClaudeClaude sees what this editor shows (view, selection, what you hover) and can take a snapshot of your screen. Changes Claude makes appear here straight away and ⌘Z undoes them. N pins a note on any spot for Claude.
SavingEvery change saves by itself to the Mac. ⌘S or “Save version” keeps a named version; “History” restores any version.
PhysicsSpeeds come from riders starting each roll-in at 1, 4 and 8 m/s (the checker’s model). Jumps show lip speed, air and where you land; the Problems tab lists every stall, short landing, hard landing, gap and dead end.

History

Every save is kept as a version every few minutes, plus each named version. Restoring loads it into the editor; you can undo that.
