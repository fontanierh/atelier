03 / MAKE IT SOLID

The flicker needs
a geometry pass.

A nicer texture cannot fix terrain lying on the water plane. Build a clear dry edge, a wet transition and a submerged toe as one continuous surface.

0.92 km²

North-country terrain at exactly sea level.

The surveyed country grid contains 276 flat quads at z=0, covering approximately 916,210 m². The sea plane is also at z=0. This is a confirmed geometry conflict risk and a plausible cause of the reported distant flicker. An in-game A/B is still needed to establish its contribution.

Schematic profile · Beach widths and cliff heights vary by stretch.

01

Replace the flat shelves.

Shape Belmonte’s east, west and north shores through sea level. Carry the seabed below water instead of leaving broad zero-height triangles. Keep the imported valley and inland roads intact.

02

Close the outer terrain.

The far hills stop at a 6.4 km square with elevated open edges. Rework the perimeter into broad headlands, close the cliff faces and sink their toes. Stitch joins to the detailed terrain.

03

Give water one clear owner.

Audit the harbor sheet, currently 2.5 cm above the main sea. Use a shared surface or a clean ownership boundary. Sand belongs on the ground surface; avoid thin duplicate shore ribbons.

04

Hold the shape at distance.

Use continuous ground and broad material bands, with foam as a finishing detail. Validate shoreline LODs, mip filtering and depth-based surf from beach height, sailboat height and the volcano rim.

Check the shore in motion

Inspect the complete perimeter in motion, check Belmonte from a standing volcano-rim view and walk the accessible beaches. Test launch and landing on the playable southern coast. Check default forward lighting, close and far views, wet transitions and open boat approaches.

NO SHIMMER · NO OPEN EDGES · NO BLOCKED ROUTES

Recommended direction: generous beaches at arrival and Belmonte; rugged cliffs on exposed flanks; a quiet, sandy northern closure.
