# Paul's body references

`body/front.png`, `left.png`, `right.png` and `back.png` are the four views Tripo Studio built his body from
(P2 multiview, 2026-10-14). The project owner drew them in ChatGPT, one view per request, as 1254 × 1254 images.
The front came first, from the boy's standing concept and his turnaround (the HarasValley drawings). The other three
are edits of the approved front.

**Front:**
> Image 1 is the character concept and image 2 is his turnaround: use them for the outfit, colours, proportions and
> painting style only. Draw ONE figure: this boy's body from the neck down, front view, orthographic, facing the
> viewer. NO head, face, hair or ears: the body ends at a bare neck, cut flat just under the jaw so a separate head can
> plug onto it, with no cap, collar stand or visible cut surface. Strict T-pose: upper arms level at shoulder height,
> elbows and wrists straight, palms facing down, fingers straight and slightly apart with the thumb clearly separated,
> legs straight and a natural hip-width apart. Body proportions as in the standing concept: a slim boy, with fingertips
> reaching mid-thigh when the arms hang down. Outfit unchanged: loose moss-green crew-neck T-shirt with short sleeves
> ending above the elbow, beige wide cargo trousers with flap side pockets and knee seams, green-and-white low
> sneakers. Clean painted anime style matching the concept, soft even studio light, no cast shadows, no floor shadow.
> Pure white background, the whole figure visible with a margin, square image. No accessories, nothing held.

**Left:** (the right view swaps LEFT for RIGHT)
> Image 1 is the approved front: keep identity, proportions, pose, palette and painting exactly. Draw the same
> headless body seen from his LEFT side, a true 90° orthographic profile: chest and toes point to the LEFT of the
> screen. Same strict T-pose. Seen from the side, the near arm points straight at the viewer and is foreshortened, and
> the far arm is hidden behind it; do not lower the arms. Same bare neck cut under the jaw, same scale and floor line
> as image 1, pure white background, square, one figure, not mirrored.

**Back:**
> Image 1 is the approved front: keep everything. Draw the same headless body from directly behind, orthographic,
> same strict T-pose, arms level, palms down, back of the neck bare and cut under the skull line, back pockets and
> seams of the trousers visible, same scale and floor line, pure white background, square, one figure.
