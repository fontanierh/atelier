Production character model sheet for Yorimichi, a stylised 3D Japanese action-exploration game in Unreal. Images 1 and 2 are renders of Cairo, the approved player character (front T-pose and three-quarter): they are the PRIMARY reference for finish, face and material language. Match exactly his softly sculpted matte 3D game-character finish, chunky simplified forms, broad clumped hair shapes, warm peach skin, a simple face with dark vertical pill eyes without whites or irises, a barely indicated nose and a small mouth, a plain light-grey studio background and soft even light. Image 3 is the villager's model sheet: use it ONLY for the sheet layout (four labelled views on top, close-ups and colour chips below), not for its flat faceted look.

THE CHARACTER: a new non-player villager, not Cairo and not a child. SUZU, the bell-ringer of the seaside town of Hidamari: an elderly woman of about seventy, short and round (about 1.46 m, a little over four heads tall), with a soft pear-shaped body, short legs, gently rounded shoulders and a slight forward settle of the head and neck, as if she has spent fifty years looking up at a bell. Her face is warm, calm and unbothered, with soft round cheeks, a small closed contented smile, a tiny nose and deep smile lines beside the mouth; her dark pill eyes are slightly downturned and half-lidded, with soft grey eyebrows above them. Silver-white hair, thick and soft, is parted in the middle, smoothed back and gathered into one low round bun at the nape, bound with a dark cord and pinned with a small dark wooden comb; two short soft locks fall before the ears and a few fine strands escape at the temples. Age shows in the shapes, not in texture: no wrinkle maps, no pore detail, no stray hairs.

COSTUME: a padded deep-indigo work haori with wide sleeves and a cream lining that turns back at the cuffs and the collar, worn closed over a dark narrow under-collar; a broad muted-vermilion obi wrapped high under the chest, its knot to the back; a plain deeper-indigo apron panel hanging from the obi to mid-shin in front; wide charcoal indigo trousers gathered at the ankle; white tabi socks and dark wooden geta sandals with two dark thongs. Over her left shoulder she carries a thick twisted straw bell-rope, coiled twice across her chest and knotted at her right hip, its loose end tucked through the obi. A small dull-gold bell-metal hand bell hangs from a short dark cord at her left hip. A folded cream cloth is tucked into the obi at her right. Palette: deep indigo 39465C, darker indigo 2C3648, warm cream E8DECA, muted vermilion A4543A, straw C3A06B, dull bell-metal gold C9A45E, dark wood 5F4634, silver-white hair CFCAC2, warm peach skin E6B183, ink 2B2A2E. One accent only: the vermilion obi.

Composition: landscape model sheet. Top row: equally sized full-body FRONT, exact RIGHT PROFILE, BACK and THREE-QUARTER views of the SAME design in a relaxed A-pose, feet slightly apart, both hands visible and empty, labelled FRONT, RIGHT PROFILE, BACK, THREE-QUARTER. Bottom row: a larger face close-up in front and in three-quarter (the half-lidded eyes, smile and bun clearly readable), a close-up of the hand bell and rope knot, a close-up of the bun with its comb, and 6 flat colour chips. Consistent height and proportions in every view. Clean modelable shapes for a game mesh rigged with a standard humanoid skeleton: no tiny straps, laces, embroidery, fine fringe or texture noise; no cloth hanging below the knees except the trousers.

No text other than the short view labels, no logos, no HUD, no border around the whole sheet, no other characters, no Cairo, no yellow pullover, no spirits or creatures, no blood, no realistic skin or cloth shading, no cropped or floor-length kimono, no umbrella, no cat, no staff.
