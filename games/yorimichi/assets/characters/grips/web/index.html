How it works

  1. Pick a grip on the left: the sword or the glider, right or left hand. Each shows Modori's hand exactly where the game holds it.
  2. Drag a coloured dot. The dot at a fingertip bends the whole finger to follow it; a dot at a joint bends the joints before it. Drag empty space to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  3. Watch the colours on the skin: red is skin inside the handle, green is touching it. Aim for green on the pads and no red.
  4. Fine-tune with the sliders: pick a finger (its chip, or a click on its dot), then bend, spread or twist each joint.
  5. On the glider the hands move a little during the glide. Scrub the moments at the bottom; the bar chart shows how deep the skin goes into the handle at each one.
  6. Mark the grip done when it looks right. Everything saves automatically as you go; nothing is lost if you close the page.

Keys: Z undo, Shift+Z redo, H hide the prop, 1–5 pick index … thumb.
