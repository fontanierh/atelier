# Transport sounds

One bank for the three passenger vehicles: the ferry, the gondola lines and the zeppelin. It has 10 loops and 15
one-shot cues (46 files). Every sound is synthesised by `make.py`, and some are layered over four Sonniss GDC
masters that the combat bank already fetches. Nothing is generated by AI, and no music is used. This folder holds
the recipe. The sounds are built into `build/yorimichi/audio/transport/`.

## Build

```sh
atelier fetch yorimichi                        # the Sonniss combat masters, md5-checked
atelier build yorimichi audio.transport        # every WAV and manifest.json (about 3 s, numpy only)
atelier build yorimichi unreal.sounds          # import into /Game/Audio/Transport
```

`make.py` uses the skateboard's DSP helpers and the bike's `ring` and `rattle`. Its output is the same on every
run, because every seed is fixed. `unreal/Scripts/import_transport_audio.py` imports each file as
`/Game/Audio/Transport/<cue>_<nn>` and sets the manifest's loops to loop.

`manifest.json` lists for each cue:

- its files, with length, peak and loudest-400 ms level;
- its `sources`: `synthesis`, plus each Sonniss master with its archive item, path and md5.

**Loops never click at the seam.** Noise is shaped in the frequency domain. Tones are snapped to whole cycles per
loop, and every train of hits wraps around the loop's end.

**Levels.** Every file sits inside the bike's and the skateboard's range. Loops peak between −9 and −4 dBFS.
One-shots peak between −9 and −1 dBFS. The game balances each sound further with its own gains.

## Runtime

`Source/Yorimichi/TransportAudio.h` holds `UTransportAudioComponent` and one subclass per vehicle. Attach a component
where the sound comes from, then drive it from the vehicle's state.

**Every machine drives its own copy.** The host and each client call the same methods from the replicated or
deterministic state, so every player hears the same thing. Nothing in this audio replicates.

**Loops fade.** The vehicle's state sets each loop's target volume and pitch every tick. The loop fades and glides
towards them, and stops only once it is silent.

**One-shots fire on transitions.** The first state a component sees only primes it, so a player who joins late
hears no stale cue.

**Attenuation.** Every sound is spatialised. It is at full volume inside an inner radius, falls off naturally over
the falloff distance, and loses its highs with distance. Signals use a wider "far" radius: the horns, the bells
and the chime.

| Component | Inner, falloff | Calls |
|---|---|---|
| `UZeppelinAudioComponent` | 30 m, 220 m | `SetState(Phase, RPM, GateOpen, bLanded)` every tick |
| `UFerryAudioComponent` | 25 m, 180 m | `SetPhase`, `SetThrottle`, `SetSpeed`, `SetThruster`, `OnBoardingOpen`, `OnBoardingClose` |
| `UGondolaAudioComponent` | 8 m, 60 m | `SetSpeed`, `OnTowerPass`, `OnGripAttach`, `OnGripRelease`, `OnDoorOpen`, `OnDoorClose` |
| `UGondolaStationAudioComponent` | 15 m, 80 m | `SetRunning(LineSpeed01)` every tick |

### Zeppelin (wired in)

`AZeppelinService` creates the component on the ship, except on a dedicated server. It calls `SetState` at the end
of every tick, offline and online. The values come from the phase it replicates and the RPM and gate it derives
locally.

- **Engines.** The idle loop plays at pitch RPM/90 and the cruise loop at RPM/480. An equal-power crossfade between
  them runs from 110 to 330 RPM. The RPM is 55 moored, 480 cruising and 90 landing, so the propellers spool audibly
  up and down. Moored, the idle sits at 0.35 volume. Otherwise it is at 0.75.
- **Envelope.** It plays while the ship is airborne (arriving, climbing, cruising, or landing until it is down). Its
  volume follows the ship's measured speed.
- **Phase 3 (departure).** The bell rings.
- **Phase 1 to 0 (the called ship is down).** The mooring thumps, then the bell rings.
- **Landed at the end of phase 5.** The mooring thumps, then the bell rings.
- **Gate.** The open cue plays when the gate starts to open, and the close cue when it starts to close or snaps shut.

### Ferry (batch B3)

```cpp
Audio = NewObject<UFerryAudioComponent>(this, TEXT("FerryAudio"));
Audio->SetupAttachment(Hull); Audio->RegisterComponent();
// every tick, on every machine, from the replicated voyage state:
Audio->SetPhase(EFerryAudioPhase::Berthed /* Departing, Cruise, Approach */);
Audio->SetThrottle(Throttle);       // 0 idle, ~0.35 manoeuvring, 1 cruise
Audio->SetSpeed(SpeedCmPerS);       // drives the wash (full at 8 m/s); negative = measure the component
Audio->SetThruster(Crab);           // 0-1 while crabbing on or off the berth
// on the brow's replicated transitions:
Audio->OnBoardingOpen();            // the brow lands on the quay
Audio->OnBoardingClose();           // the brow lifts off
```

- **Berthed to Departing.** The departure horn sounds.
- **Into Approach.** The arrival horn sounds.
- **Into Berthed.** The fender thump plays, and the moorings loop fades in.
- **Engine.** At berth it holds a low 0.18 thrum. Elsewhere its volume is 0.3 + 0.6 × throttle and its pitch is
  0.62 + 0.38 × throttle^0.8.

### Gondola (batch B4)

Attach one `UGondolaAudioComponent` to each cabin. Attach one `UGondolaStationAudioComponent` at each drive
bullwheel. Drive them from the line's shared clock:

- **Every tick.** Call `SetSpeed(cm/s)` on each cabin; the wind loop is full at 6 m/s. Call `SetRunning(0-1)` on
  each station.
- **Tower.** `OnTowerPass(Strength)` plays when the grip reaches a tower's sheaves: 1 for a support tower, lower for
  a hold-down tower.
- **Grip.** `OnGripAttach()` plays on leaving the station's detach rail, and `OnGripRelease()` on entering it.
- **Doors.** `OnDoorOpen()` plays the chime, and the door slides 0.15 s later. `OnDoorClose()` plays a warning
  chime, and the door rolls shut 0.9 s later.
