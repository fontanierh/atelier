A little help

On foot: left stick to move. Tap Jump once to jump, then again in the air for a higher front-flip double jump. Landing restores it.
Drag the right side to look around.

Roll tumbles along the ground in your left-stick direction, including diagonals. With no input it rolls forward.
Dash bursts forward on the ground or once in the air. It does not use your double jump.
At any air station, climb the steps and tap Fly to board the zeppelin. Tap Call ship if it is elsewhere. It flies on along its line (Woodland, Hidamari, Mega Park); tap ‹ Stop or Stop › before boarding to choose another stop. Look around during the flight; Skip flight completes the arrival early.
Sailboat launches near shore. Steer with left/right, tap Raise sail to cruise, Lower sail to stop, and Step ashore near land. No tacking needed.
Hold Sprint for a faster stride while stamina lasts. Each ring gives six seconds; Settings changes the ring count. Release to recover. An empty gauge must refill before sprinting again. Hold Walk to move slowly.

Map shows the whole world and where you are; tap a numbered place to travel there.
Back to spawn returns you to the starting road on foot, including from the sea.

On a computer: WASD, drag to look, Shift sprint, J walk, Space jump, F dash, Ctrl roll, E use, K sailboat, M map.

More room to explore

On iPhone, launch Yorimichi from your Home Screen to remove Safari’s address and tab bars.

  1. Tap Safari’s Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App enabled, then tap Add.
  4. Close this Safari play tab and open the new Yorimichi icon.
