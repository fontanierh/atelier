# Naming

- **Ids** are lowercase with hyphens: `cairo`, `fox-hunter`, `stone-lantern`.
- **Unreal assets** keep Unreal's prefixes: `SK_` skeletal mesh, `SM_` static mesh, `A_` animation, `BS_` blend space,
  `M_` material, `MI_` material instance, `T_` texture, `DA_` data asset.
- **Clip files** are `A_<Role>` (`A_Run`, `A_SwordAttack1`).
- **Sound cues** are dotted: `footstep.grass`, `skate.pop`, `combat.parry`.
- **Manifests** are TOML next to what they describe: `game.toml`, `character.toml`, `bank.toml`, `asset.toml`.
